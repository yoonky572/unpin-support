Contact

For questions, feedback, or a problem with Unpin, email yoonky572@outlook.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Unpin version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are optional; hide any private writing before sending them.

How do I save a thought?

Tap + on the current board, write your thought, choose a tag, and follow the placement flow to pin it. New thoughts are added to the current month.

How do I release a thought?

Press and hold the pin on a saved tag to open the release flow, then follow the on-screen hold-and-pull gesture. A mark remains on the board.

Can I read a released thought?

Yes. Tap its mark to open the entry and read the words you saved. Release does not delete your writing.

How do I delete a thought?

Press and hold the tag itself to open the deletion prompt, then confirm Delete. Deleted entries are removed from the current entry list and there is no in-app undo. Release and Delete are different actions.

Where are older boards?

Open Archive from the board menu, select a year and month, and browse its boards. Tap a tag or mark to read its entry.

Does Unpin need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Writing, pinning, releasing, and browsing saved boards work locally without an internet connection. Opening support or privacy websites requires a connection.

Can I sync, export, or recover my writing?

The current version does not include app-managed cloud sync, export, or backup. Device backups may contain app data depending on your settings, but Unpin cannot guarantee recovery. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting a problem with saved entries.
