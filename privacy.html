Unpin Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Unpin handles information in its iOS app. Unpin is a personal writing app for pinning thoughts, releasing them, and revisiting past boards. No account is required.

Your writing stays in app storage

The current version stores your entry titles, text, selected feelings, tag choices, board positions, and creation and release dates locally on your device. Unpin does not send this content to the developer or to a server. The developer cannot read or recover your entries.

Ads, analytics, and tracking

The current version does not include advertising, third-party usage analytics, third-party crash-reporting services, or cross-app tracking. Apple may separately process App Store, TestFlight, and device diagnostic information under its own privacy policies and your Apple settings.

Keeping and deleting entries

Releasing a thought is not deletion: the mark, original words, and dates remain available in Unpin. Delete removes an entry from the current saved entry list. Board page numbers and historical page counts may remain after deletion. Older local data retained during an upgrade and copies in device backups may still contain earlier entries; Delete does not erase those copies.

Device backups and removing the app

Unpin does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service. Depending on your device and backup settings, iOS app data may be included in an Apple or computer backup. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its local app data; offloading the app is different and can retain data. Backups are managed separately through Apple or your computer. Do not assume that reinstalling Unpin will restore your writing.

Security

Entries are stored within the app’s local iOS storage. The current version does not provide a separate app passcode or an additional app-level encryption layer for entries. Protect access to your device with its security settings. No storage system can guarantee absolute security.

When you contact support

If you email yoonky572@outlook.com, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue, and retain them only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Your email provider and ours process the correspondence to deliver and store it. We do not use support correspondence for advertising. Please avoid sending sensitive journal content unless you choose to share it for troubleshooting. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.

This website

These public support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website under their own privacy policies. GitHub’s privacy statement is available at GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies to these pages. Opening the website does not send your Unpin entries to us.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if Unpin’s data practices change. The updated policy will show a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact yoonky572@outlook.com.
